Next.js with the App Router is a solid choice for a developer portfolio: static generation, great DX, and easy deployment on Vercel.

## Why App Router?

- **Server Components by default** — less JS shipped, faster loads.
- **Layouts and nested routes** — shared header/footer without prop drilling.
- **Metadata API** — SEO and Open Graph without extra libraries.
- **Streaming** — better perceived performance when you add data fetching.

## TypeScript

Strong typing catches bugs early and makes refactors safer. With path aliases like `@/components`, imports stay clean.

## What's next?

Add more projects to `data/projects.ts`, drop MDX files into `content/thoughts/`, and point the contact form to your preferred backend (Formspree, Resend, or a serverless function). Then deploy to Vercel with one click.
